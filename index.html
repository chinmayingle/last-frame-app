The Last Frame

Channel publishing tool (Google OAuth app name: “story”)

This is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Last Frame. It uploads the owner's own animated short films to that channel and manages them (titles, descriptions, visibility, schedules, thumbnails) through the YouTube Data API, and reads the channel's statistics.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
